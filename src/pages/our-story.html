Why wood?

Wood is warm in the hand and different every time. A pen blank, a bowl blank, or a board starts with grain that took years to grow, and no two are the same.

It’s also practical. Cared for properly, wood holds up to daily use, and small marks become part of its story.

{{> placeholder "Close-up of figured grain on a finished bowl" "portrait"}}

The mark of handmade

Because every piece is made from real wood, the one you receive will differ from the photos. Grain lines, color, and figure change from board to board and even across a single piece. Small natural features, such as a mineral streak or a shift in tone, are part of the wood, not flaws.

If you have a preference, like lighter or darker wood, let Ed know before you order and he’ll do his best to match it.
