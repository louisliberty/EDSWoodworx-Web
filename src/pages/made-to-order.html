Made to Order

Every EDS WoodWorx piece is made after you order it. Here’s what that means, how long it takes, and what to expect.

What “made to order” means

When you place an order, Ed selects the wood and makes your piece by hand: turning or cutting, shaping, sanding, finishing, and a final check. Nothing comes from a warehouse shelf. Your piece is made for you.

Why it takes a little longer

Mass-produced goods are made ahead and stored. Handwork happens one piece at a time, and some steps can’t be rushed, like letting a finish dry properly before a piece is packed. The wait is part of getting something made with care.

Order and shipping updates

You’ll receive a receipt by email as soon as you pay. When your piece ships, Ed will email you with a tracking number. If anything affects your timing, he’ll email you directly. You can also contact Ed anytime with your receipt number.

Every piece is a little different

Real wood varies. The grain, color, and figure of your piece will differ from the photos, and that’s what makes it yours. Product photos show the style, shape, and finish you can expect.
