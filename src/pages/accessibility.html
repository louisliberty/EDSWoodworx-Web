Accessibility Statement

EDS WoodWorx wants everyone to be able to browse and buy from this site, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification, or other assistive technology.

What we’ve done

  • Designed the site to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA, as a working goal.
  • Used readable text sizes, strong color contrast, and visible keyboard focus.
  • Labeled form fields and written error messages that say how to fix the problem.
  • Added descriptive text to product and workshop photos.
  • Respected your device’s reduced-motion setting.
