# 8. Policy Page Templates

> **OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE LAUNCH.** These are starting templates, not legal advice and not final policies. Every [BRACKET] is a business decision Ed must make. Have an attorney review the final text, especially the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, which depend on where you sell and what tools you use.
